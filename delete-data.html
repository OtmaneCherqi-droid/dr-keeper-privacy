How to delete your data

Dr.Keeper stores all data locally on your device. To delete your data:

  • Delete a specific patient record: Open the patient's profile inside the app and use the delete option.
  • Delete a note or session: Swipe or tap the delete icon next to the item inside the app.
  • Delete all data at once: Uninstall the app from your device. This permanently removes the local database and all patient records.
  • Delete an exported backup file: If you shared or saved a backup file (e.g. via WhatsApp, email, or Google Drive), delete it directly from that location.

What is deleted

Deleting a record or uninstalling the app permanently removes that data from the device. Dr.Keeper does not retain any copy of your data on any server, as all data is stored locally only.

Retention

No additional retention period applies. Once deleted locally or the app is uninstalled, the data is not recoverable by the developer, as the developer never had access to it.
