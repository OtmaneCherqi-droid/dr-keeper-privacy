Privacy Policy for Dr.Keeper

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Developer: Otmane Cherqi — Contact: otmanserki@gmail.com

1. Overview

Dr.Keeper is an app designed for physicians to organize and keep records of their patients. The app does not provide medical diagnosis or advice, and does not replace professional medical consultation.

2. Data the App Processes

The app stores data entered by the physician, which may include: patient name, phone number, national ID number, date of birth, city, weight and height, diagnosis, treatment plan, treatment sessions, medical notes, and pathology/anapath images.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored locally on your device in a database, and sensitive fields are encrypted. We do not operate any servers, we do not access your data, and we do not sell or share it with any third party.

4. Backup

If you choose to create a backup or upload it to Google Drive, a file containing your data will be created. You are responsible for protecting this file and your Google account. Storage on Google Drive is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan codes and photograph documents.
  • Notifications: used to remind you of sessions and backups.
  • Internet and Google Sign-In: used only for the Google Drive backup feature.

6. Physician Responsibility

You are responsible for obtaining your patients' consent and for complying with applicable health data protection laws in your country.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete any record from within the app, or delete all data by uninstalling the app. You can also delete backups directly from Google Drive.

8. Children

This app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top will reflect the most recent update.
